under the light
of the full moon
seven will rise

was meditating
met a princess of nazareth
knelt and kissed the back of her hand
an orb of white light enveloped her
she rose into the air
got full body chills

hurts to see how far ive fallen
been in texas over half a year now
i look back at the few photos ive taken
seems a tragic decline
spiritually and physically
oh i have no shortage of excuses
but theyre empty and irrelevant
make no mistake
ive fallen before
but never like this
when i was young i learned
to make a home at rock bottom
the only stability ive known
fears have no hold when i let go

the buddha said that suffering
was caused by desire
wed learned
and that the cessation of desire
meant the cessation of suffering
when you stopped wishing
things wouldnt fall apart
youd stop suffering when they did
john green, looking for alaska

i feel so messy
unworthy of love
beneath my connections
i know the return is easy
in theory that is
but i keep digging deeper
i blame money
lack of it
and its not blameless
but blame is a silly game
it hurts seeing myself here
i dont wish this for myself
i look in the mirror
cross my eyes
and blur my vision
i find a little love
hollow little love
like my old report cards at school
blank, cause i skipped all classes
but on the back it always says
"has potential"
